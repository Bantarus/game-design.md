## Evidence: what the dogfood studies measured

v0.4's evidence comes from the dogfood harness (spec §11.3, D-023). Headless coding-agent sessions (`claude-sonnet-5-5`) run fixed tasks on isolated copies of this repository, deterministic checkers grade them, and locked rules, committed before any data, turn the results into verdicts. n is small (3 repeats per cell) and there is one subject model, so every number below is descriptive of these runs.

### Projected views (`gdmd view` / `gdmd graph`)

The two studies are always cited together (D-032).

- **Study 1, Rule V: NULL** on this repository's own trees, which are small (tens of KB).
  - The v0.4 world with the views arm consumed a median 15.6% fewer consultation bytes than the v0.3 baseline, against a 30% threshold.
  - [`docs/case-studies/dogfood-01.md`](../case-studies/dogfood-01.md).
- **Study 2, Rule V2: PASS** on a content-heavy synthetic tree: generated, 320 content entities, with a planted reference graph.
  - Consultation was a median **33.3%** lower, against the same 30% threshold, with no loss of success.
  - **The graph-shaped tasks drove it:** the two impact tasks and the backward multi-hop lookup read 59–92% fewer bytes, each answered with one to three `gdmd graph` calls.
  - The forward lookup was flat (+7.4%).
  - The guarded maintenance and negative-control tasks consulted more under views (−53% and −126%), from one added overview or `--grep` call.
  - The PASS is narrow: the median of six tasks falls between +7.4% and +59.3%.
  - [`docs/case-studies/dogfood-02.md`](../case-studies/dogfood-02.md).
- **Not a session-cost claim.** The primary is consultation bytes: the tool-result bytes returned to the model. Occupancy and cost are secondaries, and they carry the two worlds' difference in spec size.
- The spec makes no empirical claim about views. §9.9 defers to §11.3.

### The agent card (`CLAUDE.md` imports `docs/spec-card.md`)

- **Rule C (study 1) and Rule C2 (study 2): PASS.** Importing the generated card instead of the full spec lowers median per-turn context occupancy by about **47k tokens** (D = 46,769 and 47,158), with no loss of success.
- **Non-inferiority was tested only at the ceiling.** Every run in both studies succeeded. So neither study shows the card is safe on tasks that need spec text beyond it, and no card run asked for it (`gdmd spec --section`: 0 calls).

### Observed (descriptive only)

- On study 2's large tree, subjects in **both** C2 cells used `gdmd view` / `gdmd graph` without the arm text mentioning them: 33 and 22 calls, in 12 of 18 runs each.
- In study 1's Rule C, on the small trees, no run did.
